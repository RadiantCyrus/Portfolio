A self-initiated poster series for the return of Discord’s Wumpus plush. I made it to practice working inside a company’s official brand guidelines, using Discord’s typeface and colors the way the guide specifies, so the ads could pass for the company’s own marketing. One poster is black and white, to give the feeling of a new character being unlocked. I shot the photos myself and built the posters in Photoshop.

A self-initiated ad for my favorite pair of running shoes, the On Cloudrunner 3. I set out to match On’s minimal advertising style: one photograph, one short line of copy and the logo, with nothing else competing for attention. I shot it from a low angle to show off the sole, one of the shoe’s main features. I took the photo, wrote the headline and built the ad in Photoshop.

A self-initiated ad for Dbrand, a company whose work I appreciate. I set out to closely match the branding Dbrand used for its Damascus line of skins, including setting the name in a striking serif typeface. I photographed the White Damascus skin on my own 14-inch MacBook Pro, choosing a wood surface that complements the skin and natural light that brings out its holographic shine. Built in Photoshop.

Two self-initiated magazine spreads, made to experiment with editorial layout: one on the Porsche 911 GT3 RS and one on the Aston Martin Valkyrie. Each opens with a full-page image and sets the text and smaller images on the facing page. I captured the car images myself in the game Forza Horizon 6 and built the spreads in Photoshop and Illustrator. The article text comes from Porsche’s and Aston Martin’s official websites and press material.
